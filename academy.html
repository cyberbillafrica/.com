About the Academy

Building Africa's Digital Workforce

CyberBill Academy is the training division of CyberBill Africa, dedicated to developing Africa's next generation of cybersecurity and digital professionals.

While CyberBill Africa focuses on building AI-powered software platforms, the Academy addresses the critical skills gap in cybersecurity and digital technologies across the continent. Our programs combine practical training, industry certification preparation and hands-on experience to prepare professionals for careers in cybersecurity, IT support and digital technologies.

We serve learners across all age groups—from children taking their first steps in technology to working professionals advancing their careers through international certifications.
