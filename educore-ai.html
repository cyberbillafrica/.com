Product Overview

Intelligence for the Entire Education Ecosystem

EduCore AI is an intelligent education platform designed to provide instant, accurate and personalized educational assistance to students, parents, teachers, school administrators and governments.

It combines artificial intelligence, trusted educational knowledge and localized data to simplify access to educational information while supporting better decision-making across the education ecosystem.

The first deployment focuses on the Enugu State education ecosystem, with a roadmap for expansion across Nigeria and Africa—creating a national education intelligence layer.

The Problem

  • Educational information is fragmented across multiple sources and institutions.
  • Parents struggle to obtain accurate school information for informed decisions.
  • Students find admissions and examination guidance confusing and inaccessible.
  • Governments lack intelligent public-facing education assistants for citizens.
  • Teachers spend significant time answering repetitive questions from students and parents.

Our Solution

EduCore AI acts as an intelligent education companion capable of answering educational questions, guiding users through admissions, curriculum, examinations, school directories and educational policies. It centralizes fragmented information into a single, accessible AI-powered platform available 24/7.
