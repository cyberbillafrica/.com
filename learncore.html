Product Overview

Beyond Traditional Learning Management

LearnCore is an AI-powered Learning Experience Platform (LXP) designed to help educational institutions, training academies, NGOs, corporate organizations and professional learning providers digitize, manage and scale learning.

Unlike traditional Learning Management Systems that primarily store learning materials, LearnCore provides a complete digital learning ecosystem combining course management, assessments, certifications, learner engagement, analytics and AI-powered educational support.

The platform is designed as a multi-tenant SaaS solution, enabling multiple institutions to operate independently from a single cloud infrastructure—reducing costs while maintaining enterprise-grade security and performance.

The Problem

  • Many African educational institutions still rely on manual administration and physical classrooms.
  • Existing LMS platforms are often expensive, difficult to customize or designed for markets outside Africa.
  • Institutions struggle with fragmented digital tools that don't integrate seamlessly.
  • Limited access to AI-powered educational support for learners and instructors.

Our Solution

LearnCore provides an integrated platform that enables institutions to create, deliver and manage digital learning while improving learner engagement and operational efficiency through automation and artificial intelligence. With multi-tenant architecture, institutions can be onboarded rapidly without compromising security or performance.
