Government

Enugu CSDA Digital Platform

Objective Digitize agency operations for improved project management and community engagement.
Client Enugu State Community and Social Development Agency
Challenge

The agency relied on manual processes for project tracking across multiple community development initiatives. Paper-based documentation made it difficult to monitor progress, generate reports and maintain transparency with stakeholders including the World Bank.

Solution

Designed and deployed a comprehensive digital platform for end-to-end project lifecycle management. The system digitized application processing, project monitoring, fund disbursement tracking and automated reporting—all accessible to field officers via mobile devices.

Outcome

Improved project visibility across all community sites, reduced reporting time by over 60%, enhanced community participation through digital engagement tools, and strengthened accountability to development partners.

Web Application Database Management Workflow Automation Mobile-First Reporting Engine

Political Technology

Political Campaign Platforms

Objective Digitize campaign operations for improved coordination and voter engagement.
Client Multiple Political Organizations
Challenge

Political campaigns across Nigeria struggled with fragmented coordination tools. Volunteer management relied on informal channels, campaign data was scattered and measuring field performance was nearly impossible without integrated systems.

Solution

Designed and deployed digital campaign management platforms centralizing volunteer coordination, ward-level organizing, event management, constituent engagement tracking and performance analytics into single integrated environments.

Outcome

Improved volunteer mobilization and retention, enabled data-driven resource allocation across constituencies, provided real-time visibility into field operations and strengthened accountability throughout campaign structures.

Volunteer Management Campaign Analytics Ward Structure Event Management Constituent CRM

Foundation & Scholarship Platform

Objective Digitize scholarship management for educational foundations and NGO programs.
Client Educational Foundations & NGOs
Challenge

Educational foundations managing scholarship programs faced significant administrative burden processing applications manually. Tracking disbursements, monitoring academic performance and generating impact reports required extensive manual effort.

Solution

Built a comprehensive scholarship management platform digitizing the entire lifecycle—from application submission and review to fund disbursement and academic progress tracking. Integrated analytics for program impact assessment.

Cybersecurity Academy

Objective Build a digital learning platform for cybersecurity and IT skills training across Africa.
Client CyberBill Academy Division
Challenge

Africa faces a critical shortage of cybersecurity and IT professionals. Traditional training models couldn't scale to meet demand, and existing online platforms lacked the practical, hands-on approach needed for effective skills development.

Solution
