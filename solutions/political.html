The Challenge

Political Organizations Are More Than Campaigns

Modern political organizations operate across multiple structures, teams, communities and activities.

Headquarters, regional structures, constituencies, wards, departments, volunteers, members, supporters, field teams, campaigns and leadership offices all generate information and require coordination.

Yet these activities are often distributed across spreadsheets, messaging groups, disconnected databases, documents and manual processes.

The result?

Fragmented Operations

Teams work from disconnected systems and information sources.

Disconnected Information

Important organizational knowledge becomes difficult to find and maintain.

Manual Coordination

Repetitive reporting, assignments and communication consume valuable time.

Limited Organizational Intelligence

Leaders lack a unified view of activities, relationships and performance.
